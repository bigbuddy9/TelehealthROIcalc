Monthly ledger

Revenue is split into new and returning patients. Investment rows show the position after debt service.

Method. New patients = ad spend ÷ acquisition cost. Active patients each month = new patients + prior month's active × (1 − churn). New-patient revenue = new patients that month × revenue per patient; recurring revenue = returning patients × revenue per patient. Revenue share = a flat 10% of monthly profit from the patients RubyMD brings in, where profit = total revenue − processing − ad spend − medication − doctor prescription. In any month that profit is zero or negative, no share is paid. Gross profit = revenue − ad spend − medication − doctor prescription − processing − revenue share − any recurring program fee.

Financing. Standard amortizing loan on the financed balance (investment − down payment), equal monthly payments. Cash breakeven is the first month cumulative profit covers all cash paid to date; full payback is the first month it covers the entire obligation, including payments not yet due.

Scope. Pre-tax. Excludes owner time, working capital and reinvestment. Months beyond the base sheet extend the same assumptions; the patient base levels off near ad-spend-driven steady state, so no growth beyond the existing budget is assumed.
